AgentFlow AI Backup encrypts PostgreSQL archives locally with age/X25519, then uses rclone to store ciphertext in an authorized Google Drive destination.

This public informational site explains the backup integration. The AgentFlow AI application and database are separate and private. There are no public backup accounts or Drive file-management features here.

Encrypt before upload

Archives are encrypted and verified locally. The transfer adapter uploads only validated .age ciphertext, leaving manifests, credentials and private recovery keys local.

Purpose-limited Drive access

The documented permission is drive.file, used for backup files and folders created through rclone with the configured app. It does not grant general access to unrelated Drive documents.

Check and recover

Full ciphertext readback checks verify delivery. Synthetic restore testing has passed. Daily scheduling remains disabled pending operational approvals.

Why Google authorization is requested

The operator authorizes rclone separately so it can upload, locate and download the app?s encrypted backup files. This site does not collect Google tokens or initiate authorization. Actual granted permissions must be confirmed by the operator.

Current review status

Stronger key custody, retention commitments and public operator contact information require operator confirmation. Read the contact disclosure before authorizing the tool. Google makes the final verification decision.
