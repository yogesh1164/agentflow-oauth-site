Intended use

AgentFlow AI Backup is a restricted, operator-controlled backup integration for the private AgentFlow AI application. This website provides information only. It offers no public account registration, Drive file-management service or hosted backup subscription. Only authorized operators and recovery personnel may use the tool with data and Google accounts they are permitted to access.

Operator responsibilities

The operator must obtain authority to back up the source data, verify the OAuth scope and destination, protect tokens and recovery identities, restrict access, and approve scheduling, retention, monitoring and recovery procedures. Do not upload plaintext archives, credentials or private recovery keys. Read the privacy policy before authorizing access.

Backup and restore limitations

Synthetic recovery tests demonstrate the tested scenario only. They do not guarantee completeness or recovery of future backups. Validate delivery and periodically test restoration in an isolated environment. Recovery requires the correct private identity; key loss can make backups unrecoverable. Restoration may recover outdated or previously deleted records and requires operator review.

Google Drive and other dependencies

Storage and OAuth access depend on Google Drive availability, quotas, account permissions and valid authorization. Revocation or token expiry can stop transfers. rclone and age are third-party tools used locally. Google and Vercel have their own terms and policies. AgentFlow AI Backup is not endorsed by Google.

Security responsibilities

The operator must secure the host, temporary plaintext, local logs, credentials and restored data, and keep recovery keys separate from uploaded archives. Stronger vault and custodian procedures are documented requirements awaiting operational confirmation. Encryption and checksums do not eliminate all risks or replace restoration tests.

Availability and retention

There is no public uptime, retention or recovery-time commitment. Scheduling is disabled by default. The proposed retention periods and recovery targets require approval. Remote deletion is operator-managed. The operator must maintain independent recovery arrangements suited to the data and legal requirements.

Changes and contact

Effective November 7, 2026. Terms may change as the integration changes. A verified public operator identity and contact address remain pending; see Operator contact. Do not submit credentials or sensitive data through this informational site.
